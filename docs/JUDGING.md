# Judging evidence

This maps the four equally weighted EurekaDev criteria to reviewable work. It is not a predicted judge score or a promise of an award.

| Criterion | Evidence | Practical limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Innovation & Creativity, 25% | Private scenario editing, an explicit coverage floor, comparison, non-mutating disruption preview and reproducible audits in one workflow. | Fair allocation and network flow are established methods. No new algorithm claim. |
| Impact & Relevance, 25% | A relief-planning exercise motivated by documented flood impact; exact unmet need makes underserved communities visible. | Synthetic data, no field study or measured humanitarian outcome. |
| Execution & Technical Quality, 25% | Functional deployed app; independent exhaustive oracle on 300 small networks; bounded imports, CSV protection, tested closure and degenerate cases; source, setup and method documentation. | One kit type, one round and independent direct routes. No dispatch validation. |
| Presentation & Communication, 25% | 1920×1080, 30 fps website walkthrough with captions; actual product screenshots; exact comparison, trade-off, method and scope. | Synthetic stock narration is disclosed. No fabricated testimonial. |

## Judge's reproducible demonstration

1. Open the live app and inspect 180 available kits against 300 demand.
2. Compare 42/36/54/48 allocations with the nearest-first heuristic's 70/60/50/0.
3. Verify the same total delivery, 60% versus 0% minimum coverage, and 4,524 versus 3,320 kit-minutes.
4. Preview closure of South depot → Orchard: floor falls to 25%; Orchard's remaining capacity is 20.
5. Export and import a scenario. Run `npm test` to independently compare objective values.

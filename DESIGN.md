# RelayPlan design

## Style Prompt
A calm, light planning workspace for community relief volunteers. A dark forest-green sidebar anchors an ivory canvas. White cards hold clear typography, generous spacing, useful metrics and a precise schematic network. Orange marks constraints, never decorative urgency. The product feels approachable and analytical. All sample places are fictional and visibly labeled. The workflow is scenario, allocate, compare, stress test, export.

## Colors
- Forest #143c35: navigation, primary actions, allocated routes.
- Canvas #f6f7f2: page background.
- Ink #20352f: headings and body.
- Muted #5e7068: supporting text.
- Amber #ae541e: blocked routes, warnings, comparison baseline.

## Typography
System sans-serif, with tabular numbers for metrics. Body 14–16px, page titles 30–36px. Video uses the same identity, 72px headings and 32px body at 1920×1080.

## Motion
Short opacity/transform transitions, no continuous chart animation, respect reduced motion. Video entrance animations use finite eased movement and crossfades. Clear labels stay visible long enough to read.

## What NOT to Do
- No invented live disaster data or geographic map claims.
- No gradient backgrounds, neon glow, or animation hiding results.
- No personal household records, medical triage or automated dispatch.
- No color-only meaning. Labels and patterns accompany state.
- No claim that the fairness algorithm is a new research invention.
